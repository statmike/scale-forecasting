    1. `"hybrid"` — Consensus PSD similarity kernel fusing rank-normalized RBF kernels from `"physics_attractor"`, `"spectral_phase"`, `"minirocket"`, and `"structural_signature"` with per-lens attribution (`physics_sim`, `spectral_sim`, `shape_sim`, `structural_sim`).
    2. `"physics_attractor"` — Takens' delay-coordinate state-space reconstruction ($m=3$, $\tau$ from first ACF zero-crossing), discrete kinematic phase portrait $(y_t, \dot{y}_t, \ddot{y}_t)$ Sliced Wasserstein-1 distance, Rosenstein Largest Lyapunov exponent ($\lambda_{\max}$), Bandt–Pompe Permutation Entropy ($H_{\text{perm}}$), Sample Entropy, and Ornstein–Uhlenbeck damping rate ($\kappa_{\text{OU}}$).
    3. `"spectral_phase"` — Hilbert analytic signal $z_t = y_t + i\,\mathcal{H}(y_t)$, pairwise Kuramoto instantaneous phase-locking order parameter $R_{i,j} \in [0, 1]$, Welch PSD Jensen–Shannon divergence, and optimal cross-correlation lead-lag shift $\tau_{i \to j}^*$.
    4. `"elastic_dtw"` — Scale-normalized Sakoe–Chiba banded Dynamic Time Warping with physical elastic spring stiffness penalty $\nu > 0$ (`spring_stiffness`, `window_frac`).
    5. `"minirocket"` — Pure-NumPy deterministic bank of 84 ternary dilated convolutional kernels ($\mathbf{w} \in \{-1, 2\}^9$, dilations $d \in \{1, 2, 4, 8\}$) with Proportion of Positive Values (`PPV`) pooling.
    6. `"structural_signature"` — 24-D standardized econometric feature vector (STL trend/seasonal strength, rescaled-range Hurst exponent $H$, ACF lags $1..7$, PACF lags $1..3$, spectral entropy, Syntetos–Boylan $(\log \text{ADI}, \log(1+\text{CV}^2))$, skewness, kurtosis, ARCH(1) volatility clustering, level-shift ratio, zero-inflation fraction).
    7. `"foundation_latent"` — Pooled encoder embeddings from `chronos_bolt`, `chronos2`, or `timesfm_torch` when `[models-foundation]` is installed, with deterministic `"minirocket"` + `"structural_signature"` fallback (`embedding_backend_used`) on pure core.
  - Added Silhouette-optimal Ward hierarchical clustering (`cluster_by_similarity_df` with automatic small-cluster merging `< min_cluster_size`, medoid identification, and behavioral `cluster_archetype` labels), 2D spectral/MDS embedding (`embed_series_df`), and 3-mode top-$k$ digital-twin neighbor lookup (`find_similar_series_df` across `mode="full_history" | "launch_prefix" | "recent_window"`).

  - Added **inventory-aware SKU substitution & assortment intelligence** (`recommend_substitutes_df`) ranking in-stock digital-twin substitutes (`stock_status` in `{"in_stock_surplus", "adequate_stock", "low_stock_risk", "out_of_stock"}`, `substitution_score`, `substitution_rationale`) and **emerging trend & breakout detection** (`detect_emerging_trends_df` / `emerging_trends_df`) classifying series into `trend_regime` (`{"accelerating_breakout", "steady_growth", "maturing_plateau", "declining", "stable"}`) and `recommended_merchandising_action` (`{"promote_trend_leader", "co_promote_lagging_twin", "replenish_breakout_risk", "clearance_candidate", "hold_baseline"}`) using kinematic velocity/acceleration, breakout $z$-scores, Hurst persistence, and Kuramoto lead-lag bellwethers.

  - Automatically derives non-overlapping temporal aggregation factors $k \in \{k_p, \dots, 1\}$ from frequency (`[3, 6, 12]` on `"MS"`/`"ME"`/`"M"`, `[4, 13, 52]` on `"W"`, `[7, 14, 28]` on `"D"`, `[6, 12, 24]` on `"h"`), builds the temporal summing matrix $S_{\text{temp}} \in \{0, 1\}^{(\sum_k m/k) \times m}$, fits base models at each aggregated level, and projects via $\tilde{y}_{\text{temp}} = S_{\text{temp}} G_{\text{temp}} \hat{y}_{\text{temp}}$ (`ols`, `wls_struct`, `wls_var`, `mint_shrink`, `bottom_up`, `top_down`, `middle_out`) so bottom-level monthly/weekly/daily forecasts sum coherently (`< 1e-6`) to quarterly and annual totals while preserving prediction-interval widths.
